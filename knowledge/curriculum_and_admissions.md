# Janbright World School, Mokila, Hyderabad

## Campus Overview
- **School Name:** Janbright World School
- **Campus Location:** Mokila, Hyderabad, Telangana (near Financial District, Neopolis, and Shankarpally Road).
- **Curriculum:** Cambridge Assessment International Education (CAIE) curriculum offering international standard holistic education from Kindergarten through Grade 12.
- **Campus Facilities:** 15-acre green campus, Olympic-sized swimming complex, professional archery range, advanced robotics & STEAM makerspace, innovation labs, and modern boarding facilities.

## Admissions & Fee Policy
- **Absolute Rule on Academic Fees:** STRICT FEE DEFLECTION.
  - Never quote, disclose, or confirm academic tuition fee numbers over the phone or voice call.
  - Defer all academic fee queries politely to an in-person, on-campus admissions meeting and campus walkthrough at our Mokila campus.
  - Reason given to parents: "Academic fee structures at Janbright vary based on grade levels, subject combinations, second language choices, and transport options. We review the customized, complete fee schedule during your personalized campus counseling meeting."
- **Trial Workshop Cost:** All Saturday trial club workshops (Robotics, Archery, Swimming) are 100% complimentary (Free of cost) for prospective students and parents.
